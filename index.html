About the Competition

Tell Your Story at ANA

The ANA Creator Studio Award is an initiative by ANA Restaurant, part of Mohamed Hilal Group, created to discover and support the next generation of content creators in the UAE.

Participants are invited to create an original 45–90 second video that tells their story at ANA — capturing the warmth of Emirati hospitality, the richness of modern Emirati heritage, and the moments that make ANA more than a restaurant.

Where every sitting is a ritual.

Whether your story is inspired by the food, the architecture, the people, or the memories created around the table — we want to see ANA through your unique creative perspective.

The Process

How it works.

01

Register

Complete the online registration form below.

02

Confirmation

Our team reviews your registration and contacts you to confirm your participation and schedule your filming session at your preferred ANA branch.

03

Visit ANA

Visit your selected branch on your confirmed date and enjoy a complimentary set menu prepared for competition participants. Each participant may bring one guest.

04

Create

Produce an original 45–90 second video that showcases your story at ANA — celebrating the restaurant's atmosphere, hospitality, and modern Emirati identity.

05

Post

Publish your video on your public Instagram or TikTok account. Tag @ana.restaurant, include #YourStoryAtANA, and keep your account public until the competition ends.

06

Judging

A panel of industry professionals evaluates all eligible submissions.

07

Winner Announcement

The winner of the ANA Creator Studio Award is announced on 20 October.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need to purchase food?

No. All participants will be served a complimentary set menu during their scheduled visit.

Can I choose my preferred ANA branch?

Yes. You may select either ANA Dubai Mall or ANA Dalma Mall during registration.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. Each participant may bring one guest.

Can someone else film my video?

No. Participants must personally film and edit their own submission.

Which social media platforms are accepted?

Only Instagram and TikTok.

How long should my video be?

Your submission must be between 45 and 90 seconds.

How many entries can I submit?

One entry per participant.

When will the winner be announced?

The winner will be announced on 20 October.
